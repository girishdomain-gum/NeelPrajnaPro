## HC — the Owner's candidates read (verbatim, the wave's human check)
"My interpretation is that the 39 admitted configurations look more
like 2025's prevailing upward trend expressed through 39 closely
related FVG parameterizations than evidence of a distinct FVG
mechanism. The fact that every admitted configuration is long-only,
clustered around similar hold periods and low strength thresholds,
points more toward a common market regime than multiple independent
discoveries. I consider that a success of the screening process, not a
failure. The screener produced candidates without promoting them into
scientific claims, and the report correctly distinguishes candidate
generation from hypothesis validation. The multiplicity accounting
(m = 1004) and the resulting effective alpha reinforce that these
observations are far from sufficient to justify a new hypothesis
without exceptionally strong supporting evidence. I therefore approve
the report and recommend GO. More importantly, I agree with its
strategic conclusion: Generation 2 should prioritize exploring
genuinely new concept families rather than continuing to optimize an
increasingly expensive FVG family."
