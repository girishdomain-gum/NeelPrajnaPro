# Generation 2 Planning · questions for the Owner · 2026-07-26
Author: architect (fable) · Status: DRAFT — the Owner's answers become
the Generation-2 charter; ARCH-011 is drafted only after.

Generation 1 froze a working scientific operating system. Generation 2
produces KNOWLEDGE on it. These are the charter questions — framed as
questions because every one of them is a scientific values choice, not
an engineering one. Take your time; per your own instruction, no sprint
starts until you answer.

## Q1 — What is Generation 2's success criterion?
Gen 1's was "the machinery is honest" (achieved). Options, not
exclusive: (a) a defined number of DECISIVE verdicts (FAIL counts as
success); (b) the first PROMOTION under all four gates; (c) a mapped
estate — N concept families each judged to a verdict or deprioritized;
(d) a standing beliefs document a stranger could audit. What would make
you call Gen 2 complete?

## Q2 — Which concept families earn the first attempts?
Each registration now visibly costs its family α. Candidates you have
named over time: CRT, TBS, momentum, moving averages, order flow,
volume profile, microstructure. Rank 2–4 for Wave 1 of Gen 2 — and for
each, one sentence on WHY it might work (the mechanism), because the
placebo choice follows the claim type.

## Q3 — Instruments and data?
Stay XAUUSD-only (deep familiarity, existing lens/reserves) or add an
instrument (e.g., an index or FX pair) for cross-instrument evidence?
Adding one means: new ingest, new reserves by your typed phrase, new
lens export, new cost model — all on frozen machinery, no new
subsystems. Also: extend into 2026 data as it accrues?

## Q4 — What does the regime limitation demand?
Every Gen-1 negative is conditioned on trending 2024–25 gold. Options:
(a) accept and state it per-claim; (b) pre-register regime definitions
(trend/flat/bear by a sealed rule) and judge per-regime when n allows;
(c) wait for regime diversity in accrued data. (b) is the ambitious
honest path but spends more trials per claim.

itself the product for now. This decides whether Gen 2 needs the
VIRGIN-unlock ceremony designed (the one piece of near-framework work
that might be justified — flag it now if ever).
