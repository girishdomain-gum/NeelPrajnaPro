## Retrospective (standing GO-SN section — scientific emphasis per Owner)

**What the framework learned about placebo design.** A placebo is not
"shuffle something and re-run" — the null must be the negation of the
CLAIM. Wave 1 forced this precision: a directional claim (H-002) is
nulled by breaking the direction↔outcome alignment; a timing claim
(H-003) by breaking the timing↔outcome alignment; a single method would
have been the wrong null for one of the two. And the sprint's deepest
scientific moment came from the null that "misbehaved": the H-003
placebo PASSed 6/20 at α=0.05 — random-time long entries on 2024 gold
genuinely profit. That is not a broken placebo; it is the placebo
measuring the base drift the claim must BEAT. The lesson, now doctrine:
a timing claim's null carries the market's drift, so its bar is "better
than random timing", never "better than zero" — and any null's bias
must run AGAINST the hypothesis (conservative direction), documented at
introduction.

**What the framework learned about promotion discipline.** Zero
promotions after a sprint that BUILT promotion is the result, not the
absence of one. Gate (c) is unpayable until a second, independent feed
exists — a deliberate admission that single-source evidence cannot
graduate no matter how good its statistics. And gate (b) proved itself
immediately in counterfactual: had H-003 squeaked a marginal PASS, the
over-ceiling placebo would have refused promotion as
indistinguishable-from-drift. G-3 fed G-1 on the first live run. The
graduation record is designed so its mere EXISTENCE is proof (refuse
before write); the IVF audits that property rather than trusting it.

**What the framework learned about negative results.** H-002's FAIL is
the sprint's principal scientific product: it ANSWERED the observatory's
weekend question at the judging tier — excluding weekend-born FVGs does
not rescue follow-through (n=637, −0.52/oz net, p=0.93) — and the
family xauusd_h1/smc.fvg deprioritized itself via its own
pre-registered interpretation, written before the run. No goalposts
moved, no salvage narrative. H-003's INSUFFICIENT taught the
complementary lesson: it is NOT a weak FAIL. The n-floor refused to let
28 Mondays impersonate evidence in either direction, and the honest
successor path is more data, not looser thresholds. A framework that
pays this much machinery for a FAIL and an INSUFFICIENT — and records
both as wins — is measuring the right thing.

**What the framework learned about verification's evolution.** Three
steps up this sprint. (1) The IVF moved from checking recorded numbers
to REPRODUCING THE EXPERIMENT: an independent event universe and judge
re-derived both verdicts to machine precision, and only that anchor
licensed re-judging the 40 nulls from their seeds — trust in the
recomputation was itself earned by recomputation. (2) Manifest-hash
verification made file provenance irrelevant: bytes that hash to the
ledger ARE the dataset, wherever they sit — which converted a
process-debt (worktree-stranded parquets) into a non-issue without
weakening the hand-copy ban. (3) The HUMAN layer found what two machine
layers agreed on: the HC sampler exposed that my "within Monday"
geometry claim was an idealized-calendar artifact — engine and IVF
agreed to 1e-9 because both ran on the real bars; only laying the
trades against a calendar for human eyes revealed the ruling prose was
false. Verification layers are not redundant; they see different
things. New standing rule: calendar/session geometry is verified
against the real feed's bars, never a constructed clock.

**What to improve.** Architect bugs #14/#15, both self-caught before
harm, both the same species: ruling prose asserting properties not
checked against the shipped artifact (#14) or the real data (#15).

for delegated tasks (pinned vendoring, self-verifying provenance,
