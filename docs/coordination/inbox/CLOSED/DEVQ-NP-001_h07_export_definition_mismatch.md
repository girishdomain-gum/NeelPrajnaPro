## Summary (one line)
§5's parameter trigger has fired, but the reality is larger than the trigger's wording anticipates: the 324-trade H-07 export was produced by a **structurally different detector** than §5 defines — different timeframe, different tolerance basis, different pool-level rule, and one of §5's three sealed events (REVERSAL_CONFIRMED) **absent entirely**. This is a category mismatch, not a parameter tweak, so the "re-seal as v1.1 with the evidenced values" remedy does not cleanly express it. **Halting per §5 before any registration, run, or burn.**

## What §5 freezes vs. what the export actually ran
§5 was sealed by reading the MQL5 gate `T3_SweepFVGGate.mqh` v2.1 (verified: T3 genuinely uses H1 anchor, `PoolTol = 0.15×ATR14(anchorTF)`, pool level = **average** of member pivots, gap-through exclusion, and a mandatory MSS stage — §5 is a *faithful* abstraction of T3). The 324 trades, however, were generated by the **Python bespoke stack** `np_probability_engine.py` + `np_feature_service.py` (which §4/§5 label "reference only, retired from evidentiary service"). Those never touched T3.

| 1 | anchor TF **H1** | single **M5 (300 s)** timeframe (`BAR_SECS=300`) | MISMATCH |

Tally: 2 nominal matches, 8 mismatches (3 of them structural). Three independent records agree the export is M5/fixed-tol: the generating code (`BAR_SECS=300`, `build_pools_and_sweeps(bars, swings, 30.0, 5.0, 3, 2)`, entry `= bar_ts + BAR_SECS`), `Validation/Stage3/Stage3_FeatureValidationReport.json` (`timeframe_s 300`, `pool_tol_ticks 30`), and `knowledge_base/kb.json` ("on XAUUSD M5").

### Event-by-event (deliverable-5 mapping, sealed here before any run)
- **POOL_FORMED — DIVERGENT:** level = max/min not average; fixed 30-tick tol not `0.15×ATR14(H1)`; ~200 M5-bar window not 500 H1 bars.
- **SWEEP — DIVERGENT:** imposes a ≥5-tick minimum penetration and a **2-bar** reclose window (§5 = a single closed bar); the detector never reads the bar **open** (`h,l,c` only), so §5's "opens inside the defended side" requirement **and** the gap-through-open exclusion are both unenforceable.
- **REVERSAL_CONFIRMED — ABSENT:** no swing_lookback, no mss_max_bars, no "closes beyond pre-sweep swing" logic. 325 sweeps → 324 trades (one dropped for degenerate geometry): essentially every sweep trades. The E2 claim's declared testable content — "does REVERSAL_CONFIRMED follow SWEEP beyond chance timing?" — is **not measured anywhere in the population** the sprint intends to judge.

## Why the §5 "re-seal as v1.1 with the evidenced values" remedy does not cleanly apply
§5's trigger presumes the export came from the **same T3 gate** with *non-default input values*. It did not — it came from a different engine on a different timeframe with a **missing event**. "Re-seal with the evidenced values" cannot express "the export has no MSS stage at all" or "the export is single-TF M5 with a fixed absolute tolerance," because those are not *values* of §5's parameters — they describe a different detector. A developer following §5 literally finds a category mismatch, not a parameter substitution. Per Constitution P5 (append-only; corrections are new records) and §6/§7.3 (freeze/amendment is permanently-human, via NP-ADR ratified by the Owner), this cannot be silently reconciled by the Developer.

## What this blocks
- **Deliverable 1** (build the §5-faithful detector): buildable, but a §5-faithful detector generates a **different event/trade set** than the 324 (different TF, adaptive tol, plus an MSS filter that makes trades a *subset* of sweeps).
- **Deliverable 4 / AC-3** ("one real `EvidenceBattery.run()` … over **the same 324-trade population** B1–B7 judged"): the premise is unsatisfiable as written — a §5 detector cannot reproduce the 324, and the 324 were not made by a §5 detector.
- **Deliverable 5 / AC-4** (nine-steps vs B1–B7 gate-by-gate): the two instruments no longer share an underlying population, so the comparison would compare a §5 detector's verdict against a *non-§5* population's recorded verdict.
- **Deliverable 3** (the two sealed registrations, incl. the E2 whose testable content is REVERSAL_CONFIRMED): can't seal an Observation Space asserting "H1 pools / M1 events" against an M5 population with no reversal step.

## Options (for the Architect to rule, Owner to ratify where it touches the seal)
- **A — Re-run the population under §5.** Build the §5-faithful detector (deliverable 1) and generate a fresh H-07 population on the designated window under H1/M1 + adaptive tol + REVERSAL_CONFIRMED; judge *that* with the real Battery. Consequence: deliverable-4's "same 324-trade population" is replaced by "the §5 population over the same window"; AC-4 compares §5-real-Battery vs the recorded bespoke B1–B7 as two *different instruments over the same market time* (still a valid, informative comparison — divergence is the point). Also changes the burn footprint (see DEVQ-NP-002: a §5 detector consumes up to 500 H1 bars of pre-roll).
- **B — Re-seal the definition as v1.1 to *match the evidenced export* (M5, fixed 30-tick tol, max/min level, no MSS).** This honours "judge the same 324 population" literally, but it abandons §5's faithful capture of the real EA gate T3 and drops the REVERSAL_CONFIRMED event that gives the E2 claim its content. Requires an NP-ADR + Owner ratification (§7.3). I flag this as scientifically weaker — it defines H-07 as whatever the retired tool happened to compute.
- **C — Split the claim.** Keep §5 as the normative H-07 definition; register the 324-trade bespoke population as a *separate, explicitly-labelled* "H-07-bespoke-M5" corroborating artifact (not H-07 proper), and run the real Battery on the §5 population (Option A) as the sprint's actual judged claim. AC-4's comparison then names the instrument divergence directly.

Recommendation: **A or C** (both keep §5 = T3 faithful and keep REVERSAL_CONFIRMED's testable content). I have no authority to choose; this is an Architect ruling with an Owner-ratified re-seal if the definition changes at all.
