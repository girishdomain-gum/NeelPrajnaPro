# ADR-011 · Trial accounting: registration spends the attempt · 2026-07-26
Status: ACCEPTED · Author: architect (fable) · Owner-approved (ARCH-010 §1,
retro-count YES) · Implemented: HypothesisRegistry.register (S10),
scripts/retro_trials_s10.py · Verified: DEVQ-024 REPLY, IVF-S10 check §A.

## Decision
Every hypothesis REGISTRATION appends exactly one `trial_count`
`{family, lineage, n_attempts: 1}` in the same flow, parented on the
hypothesis, sharing its event_ts, `source="human"`. Idempotent
re-registration appends nothing. The scientific cost of an attempt is
paid at the moment the idea is registered — not when it completes, not
when it passes. DEVQ-015's prefix-matching deflation therefore sees
every attempt a family has ever made, screener sweeps and hypotheses
alike, at judging time.

## Why
Multiplicity correction that counts only finished or successful
experiments undercounts the garden of forking paths. H-004 was judged
at α=0.05 because H-003's attempt was invisible to the ledger — its
FAIL was a-fortiori honest, but a marginal PASS would not have been.
This ADR closes the last unpaid channel by which the framework could
have flattered itself.

## Retro-count (Owner: YES)
Four back-dated records (h001..h004, one each) appended 2026-07-26 as
ordinary NEW ledger entries — history is never rewritten; the ledger
learns. h001 (schema v1, no family field) counts under
`xauusd_h1/smc.fvg` per its claim (DEVQ-015). Resulting totals:
smc.fvg 502→504 (→1004 after the Wave-2 sweep's honest 500),
seasonality.calendar 0→2. Existing verdicts' recorded `family_m`
values are history and stand untouched.

## Consequences
Any THIRD seasonality.calendar attempt deflates against m≥2 plus
whatever sweeps touch the family; smc.fvg claims face α≈5e-5. Stricter
alphas are the direction honesty costs. The IVF audits ledger-wide
completeness: a hypothesis without its trial_count is a RED.
