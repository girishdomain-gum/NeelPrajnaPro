## 0. The assumptions beneath the method

Every science rests on commitments that precede its method; stating them explicitly is what keeps them revisable instead of invisible. QRF's are few, and — wherever possible — they are held as *falsifiable working positions*, not articles of faith:

1. **Markets may contain discoverable regularities at the granularity QRF can observe.** This is not assumed true — it is the program's master question, and the method is built so that a lifetime of honest negatives would *answer* it rather than embarrass it. Substantial randomness is the default expectation; structure must be demonstrated beyond it.
2. **Recorded evidence outranks intuition, experience, and authority** — including the intuitions of QRF's own authors, and especially when they conflict.
3. **Statistical judgment against claim-matched nulls is an appropriate judge** for claims at this granularity — adopted knowingly, with its limits (finite data, regime conditioning, model dependence of every null) stated rather than hidden.
4. **Knowledge is cumulative only if every attempt is counted** — an uncounted search silently converts luck into "findings," so cumulativity is purchased with the multiplicity price, or not at all.
5. **All market knowledge is scoped and conditioned.** Nothing discovered here is assumed universal across instruments, granularities, regimes, or eras; generality is earned stepwise (§5), never presumed.
6. **A claim that cannot be reproduced from raw records is not knowledge**, whatever else it is.
7. **Explanation exceeds prediction.** A mechanism that says *why* — and thereby risks more, by making distinguishing predictions — is worth more than a correlation of equal accuracy; and a merely predictive model, however profitable, is not yet understanding.
8. **The limits are permanent and stated:** markets are adversarial and reflexive — regularities can decay because they are discovered; the observer trades in the system observed; and no finding here attains the ahistorical universality of physical law. QRF's claims are therefore always dated, scoped, and held ready to be un-learned.

These assumptions may be amended only as the method itself may be (§7) — and assumption 1 is the one the whole program exists to test.

---

By one method, which everything else merely implements:

```
   Observe → Measure → Describe → Classify → Discover →
   Explain → Hypothesize → Experiment → Validate →
   Accumulate Knowledge → Generalize → Develop Principles → (Potential Theory)
```

The method rests on a single honest question, asked at every stage: **did we count every opportunity to fool ourselves?** Markets are finite, noisy, and adversarially over-studied; almost everything that looks like structure is chance, drift, or the researcher's own reflection. QRF's method is therefore built not to *find* knowledge faster, but to make *false* knowledge as hard as possible to accumulate. Its defining commitments:

- **Observation precedes interpretation.** What happened is recorded before what it means is proposed; meaning arrives sealed, or not at all.
- **Registration precedes experimentation.** Every threshold, method, null construction, and success criterion is fixed — verifiably, in an append-only record — *before* the data that will judge it is examined.
- **Every attempt is counted and priced.** The cost of a scientific attempt is paid when it is conceived, not when it succeeds. A hundred quiet tries and one loud winner is one experiment with a hundred-way correction, always.
- **The judge is one, and no one instructs the judge.** All claims — human-conceived or machine-conceived, borrowed or original — face the same pre-sealed evidentiary procedure. Nothing upstream (discovery, cataloging, prioritization) and nothing downstream (beliefs, reputation, enthusiasm) ever alters a verdict.
- **History accrues; knowledge evolves.** What was claimed, when, by whom, judged how, with what result — these are immutable facts. What QRF *believes* in light of them is versioned and revisable. The two are never stored in the same kind of container.
- **A negative answer is an answer.** A decisive FAIL that closes a question outranks a positive that flatters one. Zero validated claims, honestly refused, is a scientific result.
- **Trust follows demonstration — for claims, for instruments, for methods, and for the researchers themselves,** human or machine. Every verifier must first prove it catches planted frauds; every discoverer must first prove it rejects planted nonsense; every borrowed tool is calibrated against planted truth before its output counts.

## 2. What is the lifecycle of a scientific investigation?

Every investigation, regardless of origin, walks the same path, and every step of the walk is itself an immutable record:

1. **A question is posed** — by a human, or (in later generations) surfaced by the system — and recorded with its motivation: what gap, conflict, or observation prompted it.
2. **The question is scoped.** No claim is universal. The investigation declares, before anything else, exactly where it will look: instrument, timeframe, period, session, market condition, data source, observation window. A claim is only ever true or false *inside its declared scope*.
3. **The claim is typed.** Existence ("this regularity recurs beyond chance"), mechanism ("it recurs because of this"), or predictive ("conditional on it, outcomes differ from an appropriate null"). Each type carries its own null design; establishing *that* precedes *why*, which precedes *whether it pays*.
4. **The design is sealed.** Sample-size floor, decision thresholds, the null construction matched to the claim type, and the interpretation of every possible outcome — all fixed and recorded before the judging data is seen. The seal spends the attempt: the multiplicity price is charged here, at conception.
5. **The experiment runs; the judge rules.** The outcome is one of three: the claim survived; the claim failed; or the evidence was insufficient — and insufficiency is not weak failure; it is the refusal to let thin data impersonate an answer.
6. **The verdict is reproduced independently** — re-derived from the rawest available data by a party that had no hand in producing it — before it is relied upon.
7. **Belief is updated, in writing.** The standing stance on the question — including "closed: absent within this scope," and including "still open" — is revised as a new version, with the verdict as its cited ground.
8. **The question's descendants are recorded:** what this result now makes worth asking, and what it has made not worth asking again.

## 3. What counts as evidence?

Evidence is the output of the sealed judging procedure, and **only** that. A result is evidence exactly when all of the following held:

- the claim and its full design were verifiably sealed *before* the judging data was examined;
- the claim was judged against a null matched to its type — a timing claim against random timing that inherits the market's drift, an existence claim against structure-free surrogates, a mechanism claim against its own distinguishing prediction — never against the empty bar of "better than zero";
- the attempt was counted and the verdict states its family-corrected standard;
- the judging data had never previously shaped the claim, directly or through any summary, catalog, or glance;
- the entire result re-derives, by an independent hand, from raw records to the final digit.

And the explicit list of what is **never** evidence, no matter how compelling: backtest profitability by itself; a screened candidate, however pretty; a catalog entry or descriptive summary; an idea's popularity, age, or pedigree ("survivorship was memetic, not predictive"); a machine's confidence in its own discovery; agreement between two tools that share an author or a data source; and any result whose design was written, or rewritten, after its data was seen.

## 4. When does a belief become knowledge?

A belief is a versioned stance; knowledge is the subset of beliefs that has earned standing. A belief becomes knowledge when:

1. its grounding verdict has been **independently reproduced** from raw data;
2. it has been **replicated** in at least one scope it was not born in — a different period at minimum, a different condition ideally — under a fresh seal;
3. **no unresolved contradiction** stands against it in the accumulated record — a contradiction does not delete a belief, but it suspends the word "knowledge" until resolved by a new sealed investigation;
4. if it is a claim of **absence**, it is powered: a smallest effect of interest was sealed in advance, and the data suffices to bound the effect below it — otherwise the honest stance is "not shown," never "shown not to be";
5. its statement carries its scope and its conditioning **inside the sentence** — "X recurs in trending conditions on this instrument at this granularity, 2024–2025" is knowledge; "X works" never is.

Knowledge, so defined, is what a stranger could audit: every item traceable through its records to raw data, with its price, its scope, and its surviving challenges attached.

## 5. When can knowledge become a principle?

A principle is knowledge that survived leaving home. The promotion is never rhetorical; it is a pre-registered program:

- the generalization tests — which other instruments, which other regimes, in what order — are themselves sealed in advance, each one a priced attempt;
- the knowledge survives on **at least one instrument it was not discovered on**, and in **at least one market condition it was not discovered in**, under fresh seals against fresh data;
- the mechanism-level statement, not merely the predictive statement, is what transfers — a principle explains, it does not merely correlate;
- failures of transfer are recorded as scope boundaries, honestly narrowing the principle rather than being excused.

Until all of that, the correct vocabulary is "belief" or "knowledge, scoped." The word "principle" is earned in exile or not at all.

## 6. Under what conditions could QRF ever claim a market theory?

A theory is the far end of the ladder, named so the ladder is honest about where it points — and it may never be reached. QRF may claim a market theory only when **all** of the following hold, and the claim itself is published with its full evidentiary chain:

1. **Multiple established principles** exist — each having independently survived cross-instrument and cross-regime exile;
2. the principles are **jointly explanatory**: a small set of mechanism-level statements accounts for a broad set of established phenomena, with fewer assumptions than the phenomena counted separately;
3. the theory has made at least one **novel, pre-registered prediction** — a phenomenon or boundary not used in its construction — that was subsequently confirmed under seal on data that did not exist or was reserved when the prediction was recorded;
4. the theory states its own **falsifiers**: the observations that, if made, would break it — recorded in advance, monitored honestly;
5. a standing search for **disconfirmation** has been run and survived — the theory's strongest known rival explanations tested against it, at the theory's expense.

Anything less is a framework, a model, or a hope. The method permits — and the record so far suggests — that QRF may spend its whole life at the levels of phenomena and scoped knowledge without ever earning this word. That outcome is not failure. **The method's success is measured by the honesty of the ladder, not the height reached.**

## 7. What may change in this method, and who may change it

The method binds its own authors — its guards apply most exactly when they fire on the ones who wrote them. It may be amended only by the Owner's explicit ratified record, never by drift, convenience, or any machine's judgment; and certain of its commitments — human-held reserves, the single judge, append-only history, priced attempts, seal-before-look — are recorded in the Constitution as amendable only with the presumption *against* amendment.

One class of question is deliberately left unanswered, and recorded so deferral cannot become forgetting:

## 8. The Deferred Scientific Questions register

These are not design tasks and not architectural TODOs. They are foundational questions intentionally left open until evidence accumulates — protected from premature answers and from silent abandonment alike. The register is reviewed **only at generation boundaries, by the Owner, with the accumulated record open on the table**; between boundaries, no work may presuppose an answer to any of them.

1. **Is the deepest organizing unit of this science the scientific object, or the scientific question?** (The present method is object-organized, with questions recorded at every investigation's birth.)
2. **Are scientific objects the right abstraction at all**, once a full generation has used them in anger?
3. **Are market mechanisms universal or market-specific** — does "liquidity transfer" name one thing across instruments, or a family of local behaviors?
4. **Can market principles exist** — can any market knowledge actually survive the exile of §5, or is scoped knowledge the ceiling this domain permits?
5. **Is "market theory" achievable, or only asymptotic** — a direction the ladder points, never a rung it reaches?
6. **Does discovery ultimately mean finding phenomena, or finding better questions** — and if the latter, what would a question-centric method look like?

Adding a question to this register requires the Owner's record; answering one requires evidence, cited; removing one unanswered requires the same presumption-against that protects the method's core commitments.
