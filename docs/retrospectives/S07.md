## What was built
The right organ — written fresh, not imported. Belief, the knowledge release
format, the Publication Boundary, Contract instructions that expire, the
consumption path, execution-feedback ingestion, a mirror dashboard, and a
refusal-only MQL5 EA. Plus `supersede()` on the window ledger. 125 tracked
files; 167 tests (was 128).

## The sprint's three turning points

**1. The transplant could not happen (A-028).** The import plan gate found
that Fable's runtime has no thin-hands boundary to copy: `Entry(D) = ALL
enabled BIAS gates agree AND ANY enabled TRIGGER gate fires` is computed
INLINE in the execution layer. Decide and execute are the same code, and the
dashboard has live BUY/SELL/CLOSE handlers — a steering cockpit, not a mirror.

The plan permitted importing the runtime *because* it was "the other organ,
not part of the QRF rewrite." That exemption assumed a coherent organ existed
to move. **An exception whose premise fails dies with its premise**, so the
Owner's build law resumed: nothing copied, Fable a mechanics quarry only.

**2. No untouched market time exists (F-07, AM-06).** A 20,000-bar "fresh"
window was reserved as VIRGIN with a previous-era export sitting inside it.
The exhaustive enumeration that followed found spent time running nearly
continuously from 2024-01-02 to the present — one six-day gap in two and a
half years — and the terminal's M5 history only reaches 2025-09-23. Forward
collection is the only path. S07 and S08 swapped so the work needing no
virgin data happened while fresh time accumulates.

**3. The live run found what nothing else could (F-08).** See below.

## What went well
1. **The Developer stopped, twice, on things it could not decide** — the
   Fable entanglement and the steering dashboard — rather than proceeding on
   a plausible reading. Both pauses were correct.
2. **A near-miss was reported that never landed**: an export that would have
   overlapped spent time, caught from provenance alone and deleted. Its own
   sentence is now house rule — *reporting a near-miss you didn't hit is as
   important as reporting one you did.*
3. **A constraint became the design.** The firewall forbids a shared type
   crossing between organs; rather than working around it, the Developer made
   both sides canonicalize independently and refuse on disagreement.
4. **Allow-list, not denylist**, at the Publication Boundary: adding a
   publishable field requires deliberately widening the list, never merely
   forgetting to forbid something new.
5. **The synthetic-verdict safeguard is process separation, not a flag** — a
   boolean could be forgotten or forged; "never passed through
   `Battery.judge()`, never registered" cannot be.
6. **The dashboard cannot act** rather than choosing not to: one function,
   data in, text out, no side-effecting statement to hijack.

## What went wrong
- **F-07 (shared).** A-024's instruction said "if unsure whether a span was
  examined, reserve it" — then named two datasets, and two were reserved.
  Naming examples let both parties stop looking where the examples ran out.
  It also exposed that the append-only ledger could not correct a mistaken
  reservation at all.
- **F-08.** The EA passed a green suite, a clean compile and a static scan,
  and was still broken: its parser matched `"key":"value"`; Python wrote
  `"key": "value"`. It **refused a valid instruction** while logging a
  plausible, technically-true, misleading reason.
- **An Architect over-escalation.** A-028 put a scope question to the Owner.
  Scope is the Architect's to rule; forwarding it upward turned a decision
  owed to the Developer into another round trip. Corrected in A-029.

## Rules this sprint forged
1. **An exception whose premise fails dies with its premise.**
2. **Enumerate exhaustively, never by the examples an instruction mentions.**
   And the spent unit is MARKET TIME — examining hourly bars spends the
   five-minute bars beneath them.
3. **You may retract a claim that time is untouched; you may never retract a
   claim that time was touched.** Supersede is restricted to VIRGIN, unburned
   windows — otherwise a correction could launder contamination.
4. **A boundary between two LANGUAGES is proven only by passing a REAL
   payload across it.** Matching field names on both sides proves nothing
   about bytes.
5. **A refusal for the wrong reason is the hardest defect to notice** — the
   thing looks *more* careful than it is, and the log reads plausibly.
6. **Scope is the Architect's to rule, not to forward.** Stop-and-ask remains
   for silent plans, drills that will not go RED, and anything touching the
   virgin window — what changes is who answers.

## Carried forward
- **The collection thread is now the critical path.** 60 / 20,000 VIRGIN M5
  bars. Weekly cadence. Look at nothing — the tenth batch is as untouchable
  as the first, and familiarity is how this kind of rule dies.
- **W6 becomes a real requirement in S08**, when releases are persisted:
  byte-reproducibility against a ledger, not only sealed-hash recomputation.
- The EA places no orders by design. Order placement is a later sprint and
  must not be assumed present.
- Static token scans (dashboard, EA) can be defeated by deliberate
  obfuscation — same class as the firewall's dynamic-import hole, documented
  and accepted for the same reason.
- Feedback is plain JSONL, not a hash chain: `runtime/` cannot import
  `qrf.kernel`, and a second hash-chain implementation nobody reads as
  evidence would be a duplicate of logic we already own.
